# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** April 21, 2026

Thank you for choosing to be part of our community at Creamie ("we", "us", "our"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy.

## 1. Information We Collect
Creamie is designed to respect your privacy. **We do not collect, store, or share any personal data.** 

- **No Account Required:** You do not need to create an account to use the app.
- **No Location Tracking:** We do not track your location.
- **No Personal Information:** We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other personally identifiable information.

## 2. Permissions We Request
To provide you with the core functionality of the app, we request the following permissions:
- **Network / Internet Access:** Required to fetch high-quality wallpapers from the Pexels API.
- **Set Wallpaper:** Required to allow you to apply the downloaded images as your device's home screen or lock screen wallpaper.
- **Storage Access (Older Android Versions):** Required only on older versions of Android to temporarily save images before setting them as wallpapers.

## 3. Third-Party Services
We use the **Pexels API** to provide you with stunning wallpapers. When you use the app to search for or view wallpapers, certain non-identifying data (such as search queries or IP addresses) may be processed by Pexels in accordance with their privacy policy to fulfill the request.
- Pexels Privacy Policy: [https://www.pexels.com/privacy-policy/](https://www.pexels.com/privacy-policy/)

## 4. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this Privacy Policy. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

## 5. Contact Us
If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us.
- **Email/Contact:** Please refer to the developer contact details provided on our Google Play Store listing.
